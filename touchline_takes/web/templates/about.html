About {{ brand }}

{{ tagline }} {{ brand }} is football news and opinion written by an automated newsroom and approved by a human editor.

How an article is made

  1. Reading the news. Twice a day the newsroom reads public football news feeds (BBC Sport, The Guardian and Sky Sports), results data from Football-Data.co.uk and fixtures from football-data.org.
  2. Writing. An AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic) writes an original take from those sources. Every factual sentence carries a marker like S1 that links to the source it came from.
  3. Fact-checking. A separate checker tests every factual sentence against the source it cites: names, numbers, results and who scored. Anything it can't find in the source is cut or sent back for a rewrite.
  4. Editing. An editing step checks length, tone, headline and originality. We summarise and comment; we never copy or republish source articles.
  5. Human approval. A person reads every article before it is published.

See the live numbers, including what the fact-checker has caught and how our predictions have done, on How we check.

Predictions

Win, draw and loss percentages come from our own statistical model, trained on past results. They are estimates for interest only, not betting advice, and they are often wrong.

Opinions

Lines without a source marker, and the closing "The Touchline Take", are opinion: ours, written to provoke a conversation, not to state facts.

Corrections
