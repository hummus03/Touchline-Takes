How we check

Every article here is written by an AI newsroom, checked claim by claim against its sources and approved by a person. These are the newsroom's own records{% if n.since %}, since {{ n.since }}{% endif %}, updated every time the site is.

A claim is any sentence with a name or a number. Each one must cite a source, and the checker tests it against that source: the names and numbers must be there, a result must match the score and the winner, and a statistic must belong to the right club. A claim that fails is cut, or the whole draft goes back for a rewrite; after two failed rewrites it is rejected.
