Privacy notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

{{ brand }} has no accounts, no comments, no cookies, no adverts and no tracking or analytics. We don't collect personal data about readers.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical details your browser sends when it loads a page, including your IP address, and may keep them in server logs for security and operation. GitHub handles that data under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't receive those logs.

Links to other sites

Articles link to the original reports they cite (for example BBC Sport, The Guardian and Sky Sports). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

If you email us

If you write to {{ contact_email }}, we use your email address and message only to reply and, for corrections, to fix the article. We don't share them, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you (normally none, unless you've emailed us), and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing {{ contact_email }}. If you're in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes

If this notice changes, the date above changes with it.
